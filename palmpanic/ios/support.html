Tropicana: Palm Panic for iPhone and iPad

Need help? Email albenrisdev@gmail.com and describe what you are seeing, the device and iOS version you are using, and the app version.

How to play

  • Tap tropical fruit before it disappears to score points and collect shells.
  • Chain quick taps for combos. Glowing starfruit gives a score boost for a few seconds.
  • Don’t tap the protected wildlife (sea turtles and flamingos): each one costs 2 shells and a mistake. Three mistakes end the round.
  • Shells unlock the next rounds, from Market Opening to the Coral Carnival bonus round.

Your progress

Shells, unlocked rounds, best scores and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your progress, unless it is kept in a device backup.

Sound and vibration

Sound effect volume, music volume, mute and haptics can be changed from the speaker button on the title screen.

Privacy

The app collects no personal information. The full policy is at albenrisdev.co.uk/palmpanic/ios/privacy.html.
