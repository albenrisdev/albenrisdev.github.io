EuroMillions - Generator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

EuroMillions - Generator ("the app") is published by the developer "albenris". It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and the developer does not collect or receive your personal data.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device, and you can remove it by deleting saved tickets or deleting the app:

  • Tickets you save, with any notes and favourites you add.
  • Draw results you enter yourself.
  • Your settings: theme, and that you accepted the age and responsible-play notice.
  • A copy of the public draw archive and the time it was last refreshed.

iCloud and device backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this app's data may be included in that backup. Apple handles iCloud backups under its own terms, and the developer cannot access them. You can control backup in your device settings.

Network requests

To keep draw results up to date, the app downloads public files from lottochaos.com. These requests contain no information from the app about you or your tickets. As with any web request, that site's servers can see your IP address and basic connection details. See the lottochaos.com imprint and privacy page.

Sharing

Tickets leave the app only when you choose to share, copy or export them. They then go to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It uses the internet only to download draw results.

Age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 or over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
