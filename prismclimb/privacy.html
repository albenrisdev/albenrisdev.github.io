Privacy Policy

Prism Climb (formerly Same Colors) for Android, iOS, Windows and the web. Last updated: 28 November 2026

Prism Climb ("the game") has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. The developer does not collect or receive your personal data, and does not sell data. The Android and iOS versions are free and show ads from Google AdMob between games; Google may collect some information to show those ads, as described below. The Windows and web versions show no ads.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, stars, best scores, the game in progress and your settings (such as sound and language) are saved only on your device: in the app's own storage on Android, iOS and Windows, and in your browser's local storage on the web. They are not sent to the developer. Settings › Reset progress clears your progress, and uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) removes all of it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's data in your device backup. Google handles that backup under its own terms, and the developer cannot access it.

Advertising (Android and iOS)

The Android and iOS versions use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show full-screen ads between games (at most one every three finished games and three minutes). Google, as the ad provider, may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier: the Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings, or on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system and app version;
  • information about the ads you are shown and how you interact with them.

Google uses this information to show, limit and measure ads, and may show personalised ads where your choices allow it. Google's own handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Switzerland: on first launch you will see a consent form from Google, and your answer controls whether personalised ads are used. You can change it at any time with Settings › Privacy options in the game.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it is not used. You can change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can opt out of personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device's settings (for example Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Security & privacy › Ads).

Turning off ad personalisation does not remove ads; you will see non-personalised ads instead.

Permissions (Android)

The game requests internet and network state access, which the ads SDK uses to load ads and check for a connection, and the advertising ID permissions that let the Google Mobile Ads SDK use your advertising ID as described above. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photos.

Windows

The Windows version from the Microsoft Store works offline, shows no ads and collects nothing. It is displayed with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, a component of Windows that may contact Microsoft depending on your Windows settings, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Settings › "Also on iPhone and Android" opens store links in your web browser and passes no information about you.

Web (itch.io)

The web version shows no ads and collects nothing. It is hosted by itch.io, whose own privacy policy covers your visit to its site.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads in the Android and iOS versions are provided by Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
