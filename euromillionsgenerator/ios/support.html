What the app does

The app generates number lines and shows statistics from past EuroMillions draws. It does not sell tickets, take bets or pay prizes. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to EuroMillions or any lottery operator. Check results with your official lottery operator before relying on them.

The odds

Every line has a 1 in 139,838,160 chance of the jackpot and about a 1 in 13 chance of any prize. The statistics describe past draws: they cannot predict a future draw or improve your odds. Lottery play can result in financial loss, so never play with money you can't afford to lose. The app is for adults aged 18 or over.

Your lines and results

Saved lines, notes and results you enter are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes this data, unless it is kept in a device backup.

Draw data

Draw data comes from lottochaos.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0. A copy is built into the app, and it refreshes when you are online. If the newest draw hasn't arrived yet, you can enter the result yourself on the Tickets tab.
