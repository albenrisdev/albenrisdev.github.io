Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Lumina Chronos app for Windows (the “App”), distributed through the Microsoft Store and published by Alben Risdev (the “Developer”).

Summary

The App collects no personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no servers run by the Developer. Everything it stores stays on your PC.

Data the App stores on your device

  • Timer settings: focus and break durations, rounds per cycle, sound volume, countdown ticker, auto-start breaks, background animation and pause on minimize.
  • Session state: the remaining time of a running session, saved only when you close the App with Pause on Minimize turned off, so the timer can continue when you open it again.
  • Counters: the current round and the number of completed rounds.

This data is kept in the App’s local storage on your PC and the Developer never receives it. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Network use

The App is a packaged web app. It downloads its own files (code, font, sounds and icons) from albenrisdev.co.uk to install and update itself, and then works offline. These requests contain no personal information beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address, which GitHub Pages (the host of albenrisdev.co.uk) may log; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The Developer does not use these logs.

Permissions the App requests

  • Notifications: requested when you start a session with Pause on Minimize turned off, so the App can tell you when a session ends while its window is minimised. Notifications are created on your PC; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Keep screen on: while a session runs, the App asks Windows not to turn the screen off. No prompt is shown and nothing is stored.

The App does not access your contacts, calendar, location, microphone, camera, files or browsing data.

What the App does not do

  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No accounts, sync or in-app purchases.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
