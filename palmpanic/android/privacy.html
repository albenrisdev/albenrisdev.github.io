Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to Tropicana: Palm Panic (the “App”), an Android game with the package name com.albenrisdev.palmpanic, published by Alben Risdev (the “Developer”).

Summary

Tropicana: Palm Panic does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It does not require an account, has no online features, and includes no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.

Data the App stores on your device

The App keeps the following data in its private storage on your device so that your progress is remembered between sessions:

  • Progress: shells collected, the highest round unlocked, and your best score for each round.
  • Local scores: a list of your past round results (score, shells, best combo, taps, accuracy and date).
  • Settings: sound effect volume, music volume, mute, and whether vibration is on.

This data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the Developer or to any third party. The App has no names or other personal details to store, because it asks you for none.

Backups

The App does not send your data anywhere. However, Android’s device backup (for example, Google’s backup to your Google account) may include the App’s saved progress, depending on your device’s backup settings. You can turn device backup off in your Android settings, and uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes the saved data from your device.

Permissions the App requests, and why

  • VIBRATE: to give haptic feedback when you tap fruit or hit wildlife. You can turn this off in the game’s settings.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, or any other sensitive data, and it does not use the internet.

What the App does not do

  • No network requests, accounts, or online leaderboards. The App works fully offline.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs, and no advertising ID.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
