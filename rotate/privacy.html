Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Rotate – trail bound app for Android and iOS (the “App”) published by Alben Risdev (the “Developer”).

Summary

Rotate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything the App stores stays on your device.

Data the App handles

  • Progress — the stars you earn on each level, the relics you collect, and the level you had open. Stored locally: SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS.
  • Unfinished puzzles — the moves of any level you have not yet solved, so it resumes where you left it. Stored locally in the same way, and removed when the level is solved or the progress is reset.
  • Settings — sound effects, music, haptics, and the chapter gates option. Stored locally in the same way.

Backups

Your progress and settings may be included in your device’s own backups: Google’s backup on Android, and iCloud or a computer backup on iOS. Those backups are controlled by you and by Google or Apple. The Developer does not receive them.

Permissions the App requests

  • Android — VIBRATE, for haptic feedback on puzzle events. The App requests no other permissions.
  • iOS — the App requests no permissions. The compass needle uses the device’s compass heading. The App does not read or store your location.

What the App does not do

  • No network requests. The App works fully offline.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync of your progress.
  • No sharing of data with anyone.

Children

The App is not directed at children, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
