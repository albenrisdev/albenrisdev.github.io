EuroMillions - Generator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

EuroMillions - Generator ("the app") is published by the developer "albenris". It has no accounts, analytics or crash-reporting services of its own, and the developer does not collect or receive your personal data. The app is free and shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use some information to show those ads, as described below. The app does not sell data.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device, and you can remove it by deleting saved tickets or deleting the app:

  • Tickets you save, with any notes and favourites you add.
  • Draw results you enter yourself.
  • Your settings: theme, and that you accepted the age and responsible-play notice.
  • A copy of the public draw archive and the time it was last refreshed.

iCloud and device backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this app's data may be included in that backup. Apple handles iCloud backups under its own terms, and the developer cannot access them. You can control backup in your device settings.

Network requests

To keep draw results up to date, the app downloads public files from lottochaos.com. These requests contain no information from the app about you or your tickets. (Ad requests to Google are covered in the advertising section.) As with any web request, that site's servers can see your IP address and basic connection details. See the lottochaos.com imprint and privacy page.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show a banner ad at the top of the screen. Google, as the ad provider, may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system and app version;
  • information about ads you are shown and how you interact with them.

Google uses this information to show, limit and measure ads, and may show personalised ads where your choices allow it. Google’s own handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you will see a consent form from Google after the age and responsible-play notice, and your answer controls whether personalised ads are used. You can change your answer at any time with Ad privacy choices in the app’s information (ⓘ) dialog. iOS also asks whether the app may track you; if you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, Google does not receive your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Turning off ads personalisation does not remove ads.

Sharing

Tickets leave the app only when you choose to share, copy or export them. They then go to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app uses the internet to download draw results and to load ads. The only permission it asks for is App Tracking Transparency, described above. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photos.

Age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 or over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
