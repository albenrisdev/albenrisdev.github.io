Support

Rotate – trail bound for Android and iOS

Need help with Rotate? Email albenrisdev@gmail.com and describe what you are seeing, the device and operating system you are using, and the app version.

How to play

  • Slide the frame across the board, then tap to turn the tiles inside it.
  • Restore the field so the numbers run in order.
  • Use undo to take back a turn, or reset a level to start it over.
  • Each solve earns up to three stars, based on how few turns it took.

Your progress

Progress, unfinished puzzles, and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Removing the app deletes this data, unless your device’s own backup restores it.

Settings

Sound effects, music, and haptics each have their own switch. The optional chapter gate keeps you moving along the trail in order.

Privacy

Rotate works offline and collects no personal information. The full policy is at albenrisdev.co.uk/rotate/privacy.html.
