Prism Climb for Windows, Android, iOS and the web (formerly Same Colors)

Need help with Prism Climb? Email albenrisdev@gmail.com and describe what you are seeing, the device and operating system you are using, and the app version.

How to play

  • Slide every orb on the board at once: swipe, drag with the mouse, or use the arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Two orbs of the same color merge into the next color: red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, violet and finally the rainbow Prism.
  • A merge refills your moves. A move without a merge costs one. The game ends when you run out of moves or room.
  • Points are your moves left times the new color, so merge early and often. Two Prisms make a Nova for a large bonus.
  • Each level asks for one color. Reaching it in fewer moves earns up to three stars. Endless mode has no goal.

Your progress

Progress, stars, best scores and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Settings › Reset progress clears them, and removing the app deletes them.

On Windows

  • Press F11 to switch between a window and full screen.
  • The game is available in 12 languages. It starts in your Windows language when it has it; Settings › Language changes it.
  • Prism Climb uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which is part of Windows 11 and present on almost every Windows 10 PC. If it is missing, the game offers to open Microsoft’s download page.

Privacy

The Windows version works offline, shows no ads and collects no personal information.

The Android and iOS versions show ads. Their privacy policy is at albenrisdev.co.uk/SameColors/privacy_policy.html.
