Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to Tropicana: Palm Panic (the “App”), an iPhone and iPad game with the bundle ID com.albenrisdev.palmpanic, published by Alben Risdev (the “Developer”).

Summary

The App collects no personal information. It does not require an account, it shows no ads, and it has no analytics or tracking. It does not need an internet connection, and the Developer never receives any data from it.

Data the App stores on your device

The App keeps the following data in its own storage on your device so that your progress is remembered between sessions:

  • Progress: shells collected, the highest round unlocked, and your best score for each round.
  • Local scores: a list of your past round results (score, shells, best combo, taps, accuracy and date).
  • Settings: sound effect volume, music volume, mute, and whether vibration is on.

This data is stored only on your device. The Developer never receives it.

Backups

The App’s saved progress may be included in your device backup, such as iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer, depending on your settings. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy. Deleting the App removes its saved progress from your device.

Advertising

This version of the App shows no ads. It includes Google’s ad software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), but that software is switched off and never started, so no information is sent to Google. If a future update turns ads on, this policy will be updated before that update is released, and the App will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Permissions

The App does not ask for any permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, or any other sensitive data, and it does not ask to track you. Haptic feedback can be turned off in the game’s settings.

What the App does not do

  • No accounts, online leaderboards or cloud saves from the Developer.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs from the Developer.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
