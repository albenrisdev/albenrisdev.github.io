Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to Tropicana: Palm Panic (the “App”), an Android game with the package name com.albenrisdev.palmpanic, published by Alben Risdev (the “Developer”).

Summary

The App does not require an account, and the Developer does not receive any personal information from it. The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use some information to show those ads, as described below. The App has no analytics or tracking of its own, and it does not sell data.

Data the App stores on your device

The App keeps the following data in its private storage on your device so that your progress is remembered between sessions:

  • Progress: shells collected, the highest round unlocked, and your best score for each round.
  • Local scores: a list of your past round results (score, shells, best combo, taps, accuracy and date).
  • Settings: sound effect volume, music volume, mute, and whether vibration is on.

This data is stored only on your device. The Developer never receives it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner ads on the title screen. Google, as the ad provider, may collect and use:

  • your Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system and app version;
  • information about ads you are shown and how you interact with them.

Google uses this information to show, limit and measure ads, and may show personalised ads where your choices allow it. Google’s own handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you will see a consent form from Google before personalised ads are shown, and your answer to it controls whether personalised ads are used. You can also opt out of personalised ads, or reset your advertising ID, in your Android settings. Turning off ads personalisation does not remove ads from the game.

Backups

The App’s saved progress may be included in Android’s device backup, such as Google’s backup to your Google account, depending on your device’s backup settings. You can turn device backup off in your Android settings. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage, removes the saved progress from your device.

Permissions the App requests, and why

  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback when you tap fruit or hit wildlife. You can turn this off in the game’s settings.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to load ads from Google. The game itself does not need a connection; without one, ads simply don’t appear.
  • AD_ID: lets the Google Mobile Ads SDK use your advertising ID, as described above.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, or any other sensitive data.

What the App does not do

  • No accounts, online leaderboards or cloud saves from the Developer.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs from the Developer.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No selling of personal data.

Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
